# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 17, 2026

Zoomdle is a daily picture-guessing game for iPhone and iPad.

## Information stored on your device

Zoomdle saves game progress, streaks, and statistics in on-device storage (UserDefaults). It may also keep a local copy of the puzzle list in the app’s Application Support folder so Today and Archive can load without waiting on a network request.

That information stays on your device. Zoomdle does not create an account and does not upload your guesses, stats, or streaks to our servers.

## Information we do not collect

Zoomdle does not show ads, does not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not sell personal information.

## Network

The app may request an updated puzzle list over HTTPS. If that request fails or times out, Zoomdle uses the puzzles bundled with the app.

If you subscribe to Zoomdle Plus, payment, billing, and subscription status are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your Apple ID password or full payment details.
