## Zoomdle Plus

Zoomdle Plus is an auto-renewing monthly subscription sold through the App Store. It unlocks the Archive so you can play past puzzles. Payment is charged to your Apple ID.

Your subscription renews each month unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Restoring purchases on a device signed in with the same Apple ID reapplies an active subscription.
